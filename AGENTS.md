# MemeHelm product direction

- The website is for end users and all product copy is English, aimed at an international audience.
- Keep development progress, implementation checklists, environment variables, API keys, deployment tutorials, model configuration, audit fundraising plans, and other internal team matters out of the website. Put developer information in repository documentation.
- Use the MemeHelm name, angular M mark, and “Launch it. Steer it.” tagline. Retain the desktop interface inspired by kumioOS: app shortcuts, bordered windows, and a taskbar. Use MemeHelm's own near-black, cold-white, and neon yellow-green palette; do not adopt kumioOS's cream, mint, apricot, or pastel colors. The reference informs layout and interactions only. Keep labels clear and do not copy the reference project's brand or unrelated functionality.
- Present usable product workflows and concrete feature demonstrations. Do not treat a developer control panel as the product homepage.
- Show transaction details that help users decide: the actual network, fees, recipients, split, wallet confirmation, and material contract risks. Do not disguise testnet transactions as mainnet transactions.
- Examples must be labeled as examples. Do not invent live users, transactions, revenue, market metrics, reviews, partnerships, or AI responses.
- Preserve the wallet-signing boundary. The user explicitly requested the Robinhood mainnet version (4663), without a testnet product, on 2026-11-12. Develop against the official mainnet contracts with read-only queries, simulations and local tests. Actual onchain writes are explicitly signed in the user's wallet; development authorization is not permission for the assistant to spend funds or broadcast transactions. Never request a seed phrase or private key.
- The selected issuance backend is pons v2 on Robinhood mainnet 4663. Keep MemeHelm's own interface, AI and revenue management over this protocol. Replace the historical independent issuer rather than treating it as pons-compatible. Read https://docs.ponsfamily.com/v2 and verify contract code, live economics, quoting and launch permissions. Do not reintroduce previous issuance integrations. Keep mainnet browser storage and database separate from earlier prototypes.
- The pons v2 docs describe whitelist-gated launches, but live canLaunch reads for an ordinary queried address returned true on 2026-11-12. Always query the connected wallet and current fees/configuration before preparation; never infer one wallet’s permission from another’s. Pons audit work is not a completed independent audit of MemeHelm. Do not contact the team without explicit human authorization.
- The user explicitly excluded autonomous market making (including 3M-style trading bots) and cross-chain issuance on 2026-11-12. Keep MemeHelm single-chain on Robinhood. These exclusions do not remove ordinary wallet-signed trading, liquidity pools or revenue allocation; those remain separately designed integrations and must not be advertised before implementation. Other proposed feature cuts or additions are recommendations until the user accepts them.
- Pons v2 issuance, curve trading, graduation, v4 swaps and escrow-to-split payouts are covered by local mainnet-fork verification. All initial supply belongs to the curve, not a creator preallocation. The MemeHelm adapter explicitly claims its own pons escrow ledger and supports independent fixed-recipient ETH releases. These tests are not an audit or a human-signed mainnet launch. The old independent factory is archived text, not an active artifact.
- Keep mainnet pons storage separate from prior protocol factories, independent drafts and cached observations. Historical imported projects are private tracking records, never proof of a MemeHelm launch.
- Keep motion restrained: a brief, skippable brand entrance; staggered desktop reveal; and short, interruptible window transitions. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Never invent loading percentages, connection checks, transaction progress, or developer-style boot logs.
- Keep the wallpaper clean and atmospheric: static dark sculpted curves with restrained neon accents and a few small glowing particles wandering independently. Animate the points, not the lines or ribbons. Avoid pixel clouds, stepped terrain, dense dot patterns, and a large circular plate behind the brand mark.
- Keep provider credentials in the ignored server environment; never include them in source, browser assets, screenshots, logs or commits. AI drafts stay private until a user explicitly shares them. Keep local-only project pages distinct from a deployed, authenticated public service.
- Private APIs require a verified wallet session. Preserve wallet ownership checks on projects, activities, watchlists, alerts and briefs; browser drafts must stay scoped to wallet and chain. Unowned legacy records must remain quarantined.
- Market charts and alerts must use observed data with clear timestamps and coverage. Do not invent historical candles, USD prices, trading volume, risk scores or monitoring activity. Background monitoring must not call paid AI or sign transactions automatically.
- The user requested local completion first and will provide the hosting environment later. Do not publish the local backend as an online service as part of this iteration.
